# Venue-fit study: would StockSurvey's techniques travel better to crypto, prediction markets, or forex?

## TL;DR

1. **Crypto is the strongest port.** Three of our load-bearing techniques (causal-CWT regime divergence, cross-sectional rank-IC with a tinygrad head, and aggregate drawdown gating) have *mechanistic* reasons to be 2–5× stronger in crypto: documented cross-sectional momentum + attention factors (Liu–Tsyvinski 2022), no corporate actions / no PIT-label headaches, symmetric cheap shorting via perps, and a structural funding-rate carry that does not exist in equities. Capacity is large.
2. **Prediction markets are a different research program**, not a port. The closest fits are *calibration probes* and *narrative-shock detection*, not our cross-sectional / regime-conditional toolkit. Capacity is the binding constraint (most contracts < $100k liquidity; Polymarket "long-term markets" average ~$450k). Worth a small, contained arc — not a pivot.
3. **Forex is the worst fit and we should not pivot there.** Liquid G10 majors are too efficient for the size of edge we generate (typical mean-val IC ~0.01); the carry/momentum factors that exist (Lustig–Roussanov–Verdelhan 2011, Menkhoff et al. 2012) have decayed post-2008 (Bhattacharya et al. 2022) and are already arbitraged by levered macro funds.
4. **Single most promising re-point**: cross-sectional rank-IC scorer + Deflated-Sharpe discipline → crypto top-50 alts at daily horizon, using our existing indicator grid plus *crypto-native* features (funding rate, basis, on-chain flow). The +0.012 IC ceiling that pinned us on equities was data-side at the 297-ticker / 20d horizon; in crypto the cross-sectional momentum factor is order-of-magnitude larger.
5. **Single most tempting trap**: assuming "shape-kNN reversal" or "vol-surface predictor" port. Crypto vol surfaces exist (Deribit) but free historical depth is shallow and our `vol-borrow-illiquid-vrp-falsified` lesson — quote-availability is a deployability gate upstream of cost — bites the same way.

## Venue-by-venue characterization

### Crypto (BTC/ETH + top-50 by mcap; perps)

- **Microstructure**: 24/7 markets, no halts, no corporate actions, no survivorship-bias-by-delisting in the equity sense (but very real *project-death* survivorship — coins go to zero). Deep CEX order books on top-20; thinner on the long tail.
- **Data we already have**: `ss_loaders.cryptocompare` is in the repo. CCXT-style free APIs cover Binance / Coinbase / Kraken history at minute resolution.
- **Documented inefficiencies**: Liu–Tsyvinski (2022, JoF) — three-factor model (market, size, momentum) explains the cross-section; *time-series* momentum is strong; attention proxies forecast returns. Funding rate persistence: longs systematically pay shorts in bull regimes, creating a documented basis carry (cash-and-carry) that has historically yielded 5–15% annualized with low directional exposure.
- **Capacity**: Top-50 alts can absorb $1–10M positions at <50bps slippage. Funding-rate carry has many billions of capacity at the BTC/ETH level.
- **What breaks on equities that is fixed here**: No PIT label leakage (no earnings restatements, no split adjustments), no borrow asymmetry (perps short = perps long, same fee structure), no T+2, fractionable everywhere, true 24/7 OOS.
- **New problems introduced**: Exchange counterparty risk (FTX-style), regulatory uncertainty, MEV / wash trading inflating volume on long-tail coins, regime breaks at protocol-upgrade events.

### Prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold)

- **Microstructure**: Binary/categorical contracts with fixed terminal payout. Resolution dates → exact known horizons. Markets are *parallel and shallow*, not deep and time-series.
- **Capacity**: This is the binding constraint. Polymarket long-term markets average ~$450k liquidity; 63% of short-term markets have zero volume. Kalshi has similar scale. The largest US-politics markets reach $28M, but those are 1–5 markets at any given time.
- **Documented inefficiencies**: Favorite-longshot bias (well-established; longshots under $0.10 lose >60% of stake on Kalshi per Whelan 2026); resolution-decay anomalies; informed-flow signals near event close; cross-venue arb (Polymarket vs Kalshi vs Manifold on same underlying).
- **What breaks on equities that is fixed here**: Ground-truth resolution eliminates label noise. No "what's the right forward horizon" — the market tells you. No survivorship — resolved markets stay in the archive.
- **What our toolkit does NOT fit**: Cross-sectional rank-IC across 21 mega-caps does not analogize to 50,000 heterogeneous contracts with no shared price process. Causal CWT on a probability path of a 30-day contract has ~30 datapoints — wavelets need length. Regime gates assume a shared market state.
- **What DOES port**: Pre-registered OOS discipline, Deflated-Sharpe across many small bets, the quote-availability gate (most contracts are un-quotable in size — this is exactly the vol-illiquid-VRP failure mode).

### Forex (G10 majors + selected EM crosses)

- **Microstructure**: 24/5, deepest market in the world ($7T/day), dominated by central banks and macro flow. Spreads are 0.1–1 pip on majors.
- **Capacity**: Effectively infinite at the majors; large on EM crosses.
- **Documented inefficiencies**: Lustig–Roussanov–Verdelhan (2011) — HML-FX carry slope factor, ~5% premium pre-GFC. Menkhoff et al. (2012) — currency momentum, ~10% pre-cost but decays past 3 months. Post-GFC decay is well documented; carry returns dropped sharply after 2008 (Bhattacharya et al. 2022).
- **What breaks on equities that "fixes" here**: No survivorship, no corporate actions, perfect data quality.
- **Why we should not pivot here**: At our edge size (val IC ~0.01, alpha ~0.05 Sharpe net), the major-pair carry/momentum factors are already discovered, decayed, and arbitraged by levered global-macro funds with 10× our compute. Our edge is *technique novelty applied to a less-arbitraged corner*, not *out-trading Brevan Howard on EURUSD*. Forex is the canonical "efficient at the liquid end" venue — exactly the wrong shape.

## Best matches (ranked)

### 1. Cross-sectional rank-IC scorer → crypto top-50 alts, daily horizon

- **Hypothesis**: The +0.012 mean-val-IC ceiling on Phase-2 / factor-narrow is data-side at the equity cross-section's noise floor. Crypto's cross-sectional momentum factor (Liu–Tsyvinski) is documented at ~3–4× the equity momentum factor's strength on monthly returns. At 5d horizon with our indicator grid the ceiling should lift to mean val IC +0.03–0.06, mean Sharpe +0.5–1.0.
- **Why this fixes what broke**: Equities' +0.012 IC was real but below the deflated-Sharpe bar (DSR t −1.37 once 50 configs deflated). Crypto's larger underlying factor gives more head-room above multiple-testing noise. No borrow asymmetry → long-short is symmetric, not the rank-IC/long-only mismatch we documented.
- **Falsifiability**: If mean val IC on a 5-window walk-forward (2020–2026) over the top-50-by-trailing-mcap crypto universe with our existing `IndicatorGridConfig` does not exceed +0.025 with 4/5 positive windows, the venue does not lift the binding constraint and the result is `confirmed-null`. Deflated-Sharpe target: t > +1.5 on the deployable long-short stream.
- **Smallest test**: Adapt `apps/factor/scripts/modal/train_indicator.py` to ingest crypto daily OHLCV from CryptoCompare (already wired). 5 walk-forward windows. ~6 hours Modal-T4. No new architecture; this is a venue-port, not a new model.

### 2. Funding-rate-carry overlay → crypto perp basis harvest

- **Hypothesis**: Persistent positive funding (longs paying shorts) creates a documented cash-and-carry: short perp + long spot earns the funding rate with near-zero directional exposure. This is *not* novel research — it's a well-known structural premium — but **it's a real edge we don't currently have any analog for**, and our DCA strategy could layer it as a cash-leg yield enhancement.
- **Why this fixes what broke**: This is the opposite of an arbitraged anomaly — it's a structural funding mechanism that pays *because* leveraged longs are crowded. Capacity is large. It is genuinely a capacity-constrained edge in the per-MEMORY-pointer sense: edge accrues to whoever provides liquidity to the long-leveraged crowd.
- **Falsifiability**: Backtest 2021–2026 BTC/ETH cash-and-carry (long spot, short perp, weekly rebal). Target: net Sharpe > +1.5 after exchange fees and funding-PnL accounting. If realized Sharpe < +0.8 in any 1-year subwindow, the historical pattern has decayed and the trade is dead.
- **Smallest test**: Numpy-only PnL accounting on Binance funding-rate history (free, available via Binance public API). One Jupyter notebook. ~1 day of work.
- **Counter-argument to self**: This is the textbook crypto trade; every quant fund on chain is doing it. The edge is in *funding-rate prediction* (the ML paper claims +31% annual at Sharpe 2.3) — which is a forecasting problem we can use our rank-IC machinery on. The contrarian read: it's only "non-arbitraged" relative to the equity research mainstream; in crypto it's day one.

### 3. Causal-CWT regime divergence → crypto market regime gate

- **Hypothesis**: BTC/ETH exhibit *regime breaks* (bull/bear/chop) that are sharper and shorter than equity regimes because there's no central bank dampening. The causal-CWT divergence machinery (regime app) should produce a stronger sizing/abstention gate when applied to BTC than to SPY.
- **Why this fixes what broke**: The regime app's verdict on equities was `diagnostic` — the signal is real but the best-params drift across windows. Crypto's higher vol and clearer regime breaks should improve signal/noise.
- **Falsifiability**: Replace SPY with BTC in the regime trainer's market-CWT framing. Target: gate composition lifts a fixed-weight BTC+ETH+top-10 basket's worst-window Sharpe from baseline by ≥+0.3.
- **Smallest test**: Repoint `regime train --source crypto`. Requires a CryptoCompare → `load_prices`-compatible adapter (small).

### 4. Prediction-market calibration probe (contained arc, not a pivot)

- **Hypothesis**: Polymarket prices at horizon T are *miscalibrated* in a learnable way (favorite-longshot at the extremes). A logistic-regression calibration map from observed price to realized resolution probability, trained on the Polymarket history dump, should produce a betting edge of ~3–8% expected value on contracts in the $0.05–$0.20 and $0.80–$0.95 bands.
- **Why this is small-scope**: Capacity per market is low; this is a "many small bets" strategy, not a Sharpe-amplifier for the broader stack. It does not port our cross-sectional or CWT toolkit — it uses our discipline (pre-reg, deflated-Sharpe across hundreds of contracts).
- **Falsifiability**: Out-of-sample on a held-out year, calibrated-EV strategy must produce Sharpe > +1.0 after Polymarket fees. If not, the bias has already been arbitraged on liquid markets.
- **Counter-argument**: The favorite-longshot literature is 30 years old in horse racing; it has *not* been arbitraged on Polymarket because capacity is too low for serious money. This is a true small-operator edge — but for the *same* reason, it cannot be the project's main bet.

## Anti-matches

- **Vol-surface predictor → crypto options**. Deribit has options data but free historical depth is shallow (≤2 years dense). Our `vol-borrow-illiquid-vrp-falsified` finding says: quote-availability is a deployability gate upstream of cost. Building this without paid Deribit history risks the same OOS-falsification path. Defer until/unless paid data is on the table.
- **Pairs/cointegration → forex**. Tempting because forex pairs are literally pairs. But the carry-trade unwind dynamic dominates — cointegration breaks at exactly the moments the trade needs to work. The pairs verdict was `confirmed-null` on equities for similar reasons (regime-dependent).
- **Shape-kNN reversal → crypto**. The 1-month reversal premium documented on Phase-2 mega-caps did not survive on the wider stooq_us_long universe (DSR t −3.45). The mechanism (mean reversion after overextension) is *weaker* in trending crypto where momentum dominates — porting it likely amplifies the failure.
- **Relational analog-kNN → forex**. The Phase-2 win was mega-cap-specific (per `relational-universe-shift.md`); the analog would be "major currency pairs" — too few and too co-moving to give meaningful cross-sectional kNN structure.
- **Aggregate drawdown gate → prediction markets**. No shared market state across heterogeneous contracts. Concept does not map.

## Annotated citations

- **Liu, Tsyvinski, Wu (2022)**, *Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency*, Journal of Finance 77(2): 1133–1177. — Three-factor model (market/size/momentum) for crypto cross-section; documents that 10 characteristics generate significant L/S returns and are spanned by the three-factor. The empirical foundation for re-pointing the factor scorer.
- **Liu, Tsyvinski (2021)**, *Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency*, RFS 34(6): 2689–2727. — Time-series momentum and investor-attention predictors at the BTC/ETH level. Justifies the regime-gate port.
- **Lustig, Roussanov, Verdelhan (2011)**, *Common Risk Factors in Currency Markets*, RFS 24(11): 3731–3777. — HML-FX slope factor; canonical reference for forex carry premium. The reason carry is "discovered."
- **Menkhoff, Sarno, Schmeling, Schrimpf (2012)**, *Currency Momentum Strategies*, JFE 106: 660–684. — FX momentum decays past 3 months; pre-cost ~10%, post-cost meaningfully less. The reason FX momentum is not a free edge.
- **Bhattacharya et al. (2022)**, *Currency carry trade: The decline in performance after the 2008 Global Financial Crisis*, JIFMIM. — Documents post-GFC carry decay. Counter-evidence to "just port to forex."
- **Whelan (2026)**, *Makers and Takers: The Economics of the Kalshi Prediction Market*. — 300k+ contract analysis; quantifies favorite-longshot bias on Kalshi. Foundation for the calibration probe.
- **Quantpedia (2025)**, *Systematic Edges in Prediction Markets*. — Practitioner summary of exploitable biases on Polymarket vs Kalshi.
- **He, Manela, Xia (2023, arXiv 2212.06888)**, *Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures*. — Funding-rate mechanism and basis pricing. Justifies the cash-and-carry overlay.
- **(Various, 2024–25)**, *Funding rate arbitrage CEX/DEX risk-return profiles*, ScienceDirect S2096720925000818. — Realized funding-arb returns and tail risk; sets the bar the smallest-test must clear.
- **KuCoin / PANews (2025)**, *290k+ Polymarket data points: 6 truths about liquidity*. — Per-market capacity numbers (avg $450k long-term, $1.32M sports, 63% zero-volume short-term). Foundation for the capacity-binds-prediction-markets claim.

## Open questions

1. **Crypto survivorship**: Stooq's hand-curated long manifest avoided most equity delisting issues. For crypto, do we use a point-in-time top-50-by-rolling-mcap reconstruction, or accept survivorship in v0 and audit later? (Audit-followup pattern from `delisting-aware-ew-falsified` applies.)
2. **Funding-rate data quality**: Binance funding history is free but only post-2019 dense. Is the 2019–2026 window sufficient for the cross-validation discipline we apply to equities? (Probably yes — 7 years is comparable to our 6-window factor span.)
3. **Crypto fee model**: Our 10bps equity assumption does not map. Top-tier crypto fees are 1–5bps maker/taker; long-tail alt slippage can be 50bps+. Need a per-coin liquidity-adjusted cost model before any Modal walk-forward.
4. **Prediction-market terms of service**: Kalshi requires US KYC; Polymarket is restricted for US users post-2025 settlement. A research arc that can't be deployed by the researcher is at best academic.
5. **Does the Deflated-Sharpe discipline change with venue?** Crypto's higher vol and fatter tails mean the skew/kurt adjustments in `standardize_oos` will bite harder than they did for equities. We may need a regime-conditional DSR variant before claiming a crypto arc clears the bar.
6. **The capacity-constrained-edge principle pulls us toward small venues, but our infrastructure (Modal, walk-forward, deflated-Sharpe) only earns its keep at scale.** Crypto top-50 is the goldilocks zone where both apply; prediction markets are too small for the infra, forex too efficient for the edge. This is the actual reason to prefer crypto.
