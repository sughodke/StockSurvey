    Methodology / cross-cutting¶

    • Post-2020 cross-arc ranking & DCA+vol_v3 ensemble drilldown (synthesis, 2026-05-25) — restricted every available daily-return stream to 2020-01-01 → present and re-ranked. Only two arcs significantly beat DCA at 95% Ledoit-Wolf CI: vol_v3 standalone (ΔSR_ann +10.73 [+7.89, +13.37]) and the DCA + 2×vol_v3 sleeve ensemble (+1.90 [+1.13, +3.10]); 11 other arcs (relational, gate, regime_dd_gate, rsi, dca_winner_4etf, regime_vol_target, scalogram, lie_hrp, regime_velocity, regime_cwt, pairs) all include 0 vs DCA. The sleeve-sizing finding's +1.16 ΔSharpe headline on the 33-rebal substrate REPLICATES AND GROWS on the full post-2020 daily span. Deployment recipe (DCA canonical 13-ETF + 2×vol_v3 sleeve, c_options_bps ≤ 200) survives 2020+ restriction unchanged. partial-OOS.
    • Meta-allocator regime forecasting (closing finding, 2026-05-23) — across the 6-arc strategy panel (dca, gate, pairs, relational, dca_winner_4etf, vol_v3), inverse-arc-volatility weighting (B3) is confirmed-OOS vs 1/N at 95% CI (ΔSR_ann +0.367 [+0.028, +0.682], pooled OOS Sharpe +1.722, DSR-t +4.17). Every modeling candidate (Markov-with-Laplace, K=2 turbulence overlay, meta-labeling, CUSUM change-point, Rapach-Strauss-Zhou ensemble) is reversed-OOS vs B3. Operational rule: do not forecast which arc will win — weight inversely by trailing-252d realized vol. Robustness check (2026-05-24): falsified once vol_v3 is excluded — see next entry. Scope clarification (2026-05-29): null applies to prediction-layer learners with forecast loss; sizing-layer direct-Sharpe learners are confirmed-OOS on the same panel — see learned-ensemble-beats-deterministic.
    • End-to-end deep portfolio allocator v2 (2026-05-28) — confirmed-null vs DCA AND vs deterministic 2-leg AND vs learned 2-leg per locked bar. Adds 5 raw IV/HV features per ETF + 1 availability flag (F_asset=12) + continuous vol_position ∈ [0, 2.5] output head via 2.5×sigmoid that multiplies synthetic short-vol return from raw IV-vs-HV gap (no meta-layer alpha). The vol_position head learned exactly the right behavior — fold-3 unseen 2024+ mean +0.520 / max +0.882, fold-1 ~0 (no IV pre-2019), fold-2 ~0 (early IV unattractive). fold-3 v2 +1.246 BEATS v1 +1.216 — IV features bite in the right regime. Pooled OOS Sharpe +0.776 regresses slightly from v1 +0.874 because fold-1 zero-mask + fold-2 marginal IV drag the average. Architecture works; substrate is wrong — 9-ETF synthetic short-vol is ~1/10 the per-name PnL vol_v3 extracts on 2,276-ticker DoltHub options universe. v3 must short-vol on the full DoltHub universe (top-K by OI) with per-name vol_position head.
    • End-to-end deep portfolio allocator v1 (2026-05-28) — confirmed-null vs DCA on the locked +0.10 partial-OOS bar. Zhang-Zohren-Roberts framework on the 13-ETF Phase 4d basket with raw price features + macro side channel (VIX, T10Y3M, BAA10Y, VIX percentile) — no pre-computed meta-layer alpha streams as inputs. Direct-Sharpe-loss tinygrad CUDA on Modal T4, 3-fold walk-forward 2015-2025 (5000 steps/fold). Pooled OOS Sharpe +0.874 (fold-3 unseen 2024+ best at +1.216) vs DCA +0.78 (ΔSR +0.094 [−0.20, +0.49] CI includes 0). Loses to deterministic 2-leg by ΔSR −1.16 [−1.88, −0.28] and learned 2-leg by ΔSR −3.72 [−5.08, −2.49] — the options-alpha gap is real, and ETF-feature-only models cannot construct it. v2 must add raw IV surface features to close the gap.
    • Learned 2-leg blend beats deterministic (DCA + 2x vol_v3) on Sharpe AND max-DD across 4 OOS splits (2026-05-28) — confirmed-OOS. 2-parameter MV optimization on the joint (DCA, vol_v3) daily stream beats the canonical (1, 2) recipe in every split with ΔSR_ann +3.01 to +4.91 (every CI excludes 0) and tighter max-DD (−0.08% to −0.34% vs deterministic −9.55%). Learned w_vol approx 2.24, w_dca approx 0.05. Direct methodological correction of the internal-features confirmed-null — the prior arc's forecasting framing was the wrong objective; the deterministic ensemble's alpha is mean-variance, not forecastable.
    • Meta-allocator with strategy-internal features (closing finding, 2026-05-28) — confirmed-null vs the locked pre-reg bar AND vs the user-requested canonical (DCA + 2×vol_v3) bar. 3-trial grid (D1 ridge, D2 RBF kernel ridge per pre-reg license, D3 PCA-2-PC) over 14 features (6 realized-vol_252 + 6 trailing-Sharpe-trend + cross-arc corr-eff-rank_60 + port-EW-vol_60) at quarterly cadence on the 6-arc panel; model (D2 kernel ridge, in-train Sh +1.14) posts fold-3 OOS Sh +2.83 vs B3 +4.15 (ΔSR −1.39 [−2.40, −0.28] excludes 0) vs canonical +5.94 (ΔSR −3.29 [−4.93, −1.70] excludes 0); 2024+ slice (n=445) ΔSR vs canonical −4.61 [−6.78, −2.43] excludes 0.
