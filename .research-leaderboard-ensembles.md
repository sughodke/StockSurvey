# Ensemble discovery — does any combination beat DCA on the DSR ladder?

**Date:** 2026-05-23
**Script:** `apps/docs/scripts/ensemble_discovery.py`
**Raw results:** `Output/ensemble-discovery.json`
**Calibration:** `sharpe_std_ann = 0.25` (workspace baseline, same as `compute_dsr.py`)

## TL;DR

**No ensemble honestly beats DCA-alone in any deployable sense.** Every
ensemble that looks like a "lift over DCA" on overlap-window deflated-t
is doing one or both of the following:

1. **Sample-window selection.** The biggest "lift" (relational +
   vol-v3-dolthub → +5.11) is computed on vol-v3-dolthub's native
   30-obs window (2023-08→2026-03). On *that* window DCA scores +1.53
   (worse than its full-sample +1.93) because the window contains zero
   crises. Picking the window is the lift.
2. **Vol-v3-dolthub carrying the ensemble.** vol-v3-dolthub-alone
   already scores **+5.55** on its native window. Every ensemble
   containing it scores **lower** than +5.55 once a cash book is mixed
   in — only `vol-v3-dolthub + momentum-12-1-LS` is essentially
   neutral (+4.80 vs +4.76 vol-alone, ≈ break-even). Every other
   combo strictly dilutes vol-v3-dolthub.

The cleanest reading: vol-v3-dolthub on its own is the entire
"ensemble" — adding cash books pulls the deflated-t down. And
vol-v3-dolthub's +5.55 is on a 33-obs window with no vol crisis (per
`apps/docs/docs/findings/vol-v3-dolthub-oos.md`'s own caveat). So we
have *one* arc with a strong regime-favorable number and no honest
way to convert it into a multi-arc edge.

**Top 3 ensembles by overlap-window deflated-t** (purely for the
record — none of these is a deployable improvement over either
DCA-alone or vol-v3-dolthub-alone):

1. `relational-analog + vol-v3-dolthub` — n=30, defl-t **+5.11**,
   DCA-on-overlap +1.53, ρ ≈ +0.16 daily-compounded onto monthly
   rebal grid. **Loses to vol-alone by 0.22** on the same window.
2. `dca + relational-analog + vol-v3-dolthub` — n=30, defl-t +4.95.
   Loses to vol-alone by 0.60.
3. `relational-analog + vol-v3-dolthub + factor-5d-LO-focused` —
   n=30, defl-t +4.91.

The all-in invvar ensemble of every available arc cannot be computed
(empty date intersection — vol-gauss314 ends 2023-07 while
vol-v3-dolthub starts 2023-08; no single window covers both).

**Verdict: DCA-alone remains the right deployment.** vol-v3-dolthub is
the only standalone arc that beats DCA on raw deflated-t on its own
window, but (a) the window contains no crisis, (b) live infra for the
short-vol book is unbuilt, and (c) adding it to DCA *via the additive
overlay convention* gives +3.19 — better than DCA-alone-on-overlap
(+1.53) but worse than DCA-alone-full-sample (+1.93). The
multi-window evidence for live deployment still favors DCA-alone.

## Methodology

- **Date alignment.** Streams with explicit dates: dca (inferred
  bdate_range tail-anchored to 2025-12-11 over the 5232-bar close
  panel), relational-analog (2021-01→2025-12-11 from
  `idea_b_analog_knn_dwt_walkforward.py`), vol-v3-dolthub (explicit
  `rebal_dates` field in NPZ). Streams *without* explicit dates were
  reconstructed from their training script defaults: momentum and
  low-vol from `apps/factor/scripts/{momentum_12_1,low_vol_bab}.py`
  (monthly, 21-bday step, 273-bar formation/skip, start ~2001-02-01,
  302 blocks → ~2025-04); factor-5d-LO from
  `sh-indicator-r5-s1-windows.npz` (936 × 5-bday blocks tail-anchored
  to 2026-04-01); vol-v3-gauss314 from `vol-returns.npz` (30 blocks
  tail-anchored to 2023-07-31). The tail-anchored proxies are flagged
  as approximate throughout — see caveats below.

- **Frequency reconciliation.** Daily streams compound onto coarser
  rebal grids via cumulative-log-returns + diff at grid dates
  (workspace block-Sharpe convention). The coarsest arc in any combo
  dictates the grid; finer-frequency arcs get compounded down.

- **Capital semantics.**
  - `vol-v3-dolthub`, `vol-v3-gauss314` → `overlay`: capital-free
    options book (vega budget, not portfolio share). Added 1× on top.
  - `dca`, `relational-analog`, `momentum-12-1-LS`, `low-vol-bab-LS`,
    `factor-5d-LO-focused` → `cash`: capital-competing books. Weights
    sum to 1 across the cash slice.

- **n_trials accounting.** k-arc ensemble n_trials = Σ component
  n_trials + (k − 1). The (k − 1) penalty accounts for the
  (k − 1)-dim mixing-weight choice (a *single* weight rule —
  invvar / equal / tangency, applied identically across all
  ensembles — is *not* a fine-grid search, so we don't inflate
  further). Example: dca (4) + vol-v3-dolthub (12) → n_trials = 17.

- **Weight rules.** Three single rules applied uniformly across all
  pairs to detect rule-dependence: `equal`, `invvar` (1/σ²
  normalized), `tangency` (clipped-nonnegative
  Σ⁻¹μ on the overlap window). The top pair scored identically under
  all three rules (the cash component is single — relational — so
  weights are trivial). Triples/quads were scored on `invvar` only
  for output legibility.

- **Apples-to-apples reference.** Every combo reports DCA-alone
  deflated-t evaluated on the **same date intersection** as the
  ensemble (compounded onto the same grid). This is the only honest
  way to detect whether a "lift" is regime-favorable window
  selection vs a real edge.

## Falsification check — what would need to be true for this to be real?

For "vol-v3-dolthub-driven ensemble beats DCA" to convert to a
deployable claim:

1. **vol-v3-dolthub would have to hold up across a vol crisis.** The
   33-obs window is calm-bull short-vol (2023-08→2026-03). The
   `findings/vol-v3-dolthub-oos.md` page already labels this
   `partial-OOS / MARGINAL` for the same reason. A single VIX-65
   day on a short-vol overlay erases years of premium.
2. **The DCA-on-same-overlap reference would have to be the *operational*
   DCA**, not a hypothetical "deploy starting 2023-08" DCA. The real
   DCA has been collecting +1.93 over 20.7 years through GFC, 2022,
   COVID. Comparing a 30-month overlap is not a deployment-relevant
   comparison.
3. **The short-vol live infra would have to exist.** It doesn't (per
   `CLAUDE.md`: "Live-infrastructure (`ss-vol live` + options broker)
   is unbuilt"). So even if the signal were clean, the question is
   moot until that infra is built.

Pairs *without* vol-v3-dolthub: none beat DCA-on-overlap. The closest
is `dca + relational` (lift +0.01), which is statistical noise and
already noted in this session's earlier work.

## Honest caveats

- **Tail-anchored date proxies are approximate.** momentum, low-vol,
  factor-5d, vol-gauss314 all lack explicit dates in their NPZs;
  inferred dates can drift by ±5 trading days vs the actual rebal
  grid the dump was generated against. For the pairs that include
  vol-v3-dolthub (where the real signal lives), this matters
  *less* because the dolthub dates dictate the grid and the cash
  arm is daily (so misalignment is sub-day). For pairs with two
  inferred-date arms (e.g. momentum + low-vol), the n_overlap value
  is correct but the per-block alignment is best-effort.

- **The data-snooping risk in this exercise is high.** We're searching
  C(7,2) + C(7,3) + C(7,4) = 21 + 35 + 35 = 91 ensembles, then picking
  the best deflated-t. The n_trials accounting here (Σ + (k−1))
  treats only the within-ensemble mixing-weight choice, NOT the
  cross-ensemble combinatorial choice. Even with k=2 the right
  full-search penalty would push n_trials toward ~30+ before we even
  start. We don't do that adjustment because every pair already loses
  to vol-v3-dolthub-alone — so a stricter penalty would only make the
  conclusion stronger.

- **In-sample-overlap deflated-t ≠ live-deploy deflated-t.** Anything
  using a 22- or 30-observation overlap is one regime away from
  reversing. DCA's +1.93 is on 5232 observations across 4 distinct
  regimes. There is no honest substitute for that sample length.

- **vol-v3-dolthub's correlation with DCA in this run is +0.31.** The
  earlier session reported ρ +0.276. Difference is the daily→monthly
  compounding choice and the bdate_range vs explicit-rebal alignment
  for the DCA leg; both are within the noise band of date-proxy
  uncertainty (and the qualitative conclusion is unchanged either
  way: weakly positive, not strongly diversifying).

## Verdict

**Deploy DCA alone.** No ensemble in this 7-arc universe — under
honest n_trials accounting and apples-to-apples DCA-on-same-overlap
reference — produces a deflated-t improvement that survives:
1. removing the sample-window-selection bias (vol-v3-dolthub-alone
   beats every ensemble it's part of, on its own window);
2. removing vol-v3-dolthub itself (no remaining pair beats DCA).

The honest negative answer is the more valuable one: of 91 candidate
ensembles, **zero** produce a defensible improvement over DCA-alone
for live deployment. The next-experiment hypothesis is *not* "search
more ensembles" — it is "extend vol-v3-dolthub's window through the
next vol crisis and re-test as a standalone." The ensemble framing
has been worked.
