# Beyond canonical DCA — what to research next

## 0. Constraint set (one paragraph, no more)

Canonical live = `apps/dca` (Sharpe +0.673 / CAGR +8.4% / max DD −40.7%, 2005–2025). The new bar is not Sharpe; it is **uncorrelated incremental return** at low operational complexity. Hard gates: free / public data only (Stooq, Yahoo, Kaggle Nasdaq3347, FRED, gauss314 HF IV, DoltHub options chains, CryptoCompare, EDGAR, Binance public APIs, FRED, Google Trends); local Intel Mac + Modal T4 compute; daily-bar resolution; no paid microcap or OPRA-quality feed; no PyTorch / no JAX (tinygrad + numpy only); the user has internalized that arbitraged-and-published US cross-sectional equity anomalies are dead — `apps/factor`'s four-null arc is the standing proof. Anything that re-tests cross-sectional return forecasting on US equities is auto-disqualified. The leaderboard shows ≈9/146 confirmed-OOS — Bayesian prior on any new arc clearing is ≈6%, and that's the bar we must respect.

## 1. Other prediction problems

The repo has done one prediction-problem pivot already (return → drawdown / pair-spread / IV-vs-realized) and three of four came back null or partial. The next pivots have to be **further from cross-sectional equity returns**, not closer.

- **Realized-volatility forecasting (HAR-RV, Corsi 2009 *J. Financial Econometrics* 7(2):174).** Still the standing benchmark; ~2100 citations and the Corsi (2009) OOS result on USD/CHF + S&P + T-Bond at 1d/1w/2w horizons replicates everywhere it's been tried. The post-2015 literature (Kambouroudis 2021 *J. Futures Markets*; Audrino-style augmentations) extends rather than overturns the benchmark. Free-data feasible: realized vol from Stooq daily ranges via Yang-Zhang or Garman-Klass (we have OHLC). **Mechanism that survives arbitrage**: vol is mean-reverting + long-memory; the trade isn't "predict vol" (already done) but "size the vol_v3 sleeve using HAR-RV-forecasted vol rather than trailing realized." Implementation cost: 2-3 person-days (numpy OLS on (RV_d, RV_w, RV_m) → RV_{d+h}). P(clears bar over inverse-trailing-vol baseline): **45%** — this is the *one* prediction problem with bulletproof OOS evidence.
- **Drawdown forecasting via term-structure + credit spread (Hansen 2024 *Int J Forecasting* 40:409).** The VIX × slope-of-curve "counterclockwise cycle" beats slope-alone for 6/12/18m recession probit, OOS 1950–2022. `apps/gate` v0 hit partial-OOS (Pearson +0.26, alpha +0.07) on aggregate EW features; a Hansen-cycle re-spec adds two regressors and a published OOS R² >0. Free data: FRED. Cost: 1-2 person-days extending `apps/gate`. P(clears v0 baseline): **35%**.
- **Correlation-regime forecasting.** Less robust OOS literature; Krishnan-Petkova-Ritchken-type correlation factor mostly in-sample. Skip.
- **Long-horizon equity-premium prediction (Lettau-Ludvigson 2001 cay, *JF* 56:815; Welch-Goyal-Zafirov 2024 *RFS* 37:3490).** The Welch-Goyal-Zafirov update is the death certificate at <12m horizons (half of the 46 published predictors are no-longer-significant). cay survives at *business-cycle* frequencies (multi-year) but is unusable as a daily/weekly signal. Operationally: cay can tune DCA **contribution sizing** ("buy more when cay says expected returns are high"), not trade timing. Cost: ~1 day; FRED quarterly data. P(clears bar): **15%** — even if cay signal is real, it adds noise to a Sharpe +0.67 strategy with quarterly rebal because DCA already harvests the risk premium.
- **Fed-funds-path prediction.** This is a OIS-curve replication problem; the market already prices it. Skip.
- **Pre-FOMC drift (Lucca-Moench 2015 *JF* 70:329).** **Dead** — Kurov-Wolfe-Gilbert 2020 (*Finance Research Letters*) shows the drift disappeared post-2015. Do not touch.

## 2. Asset classes the repo has NOT touched

This is the highest-EV category because *every* additional confirmed-OOS class adds an uncorrelated stream to DCA.

- **Crypto, cross-sectional factor port (Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu 2022 *JF* 77(2):1133).** Already pre-registered (`TODO/factor-crypto-venue-test.md`) and scaffolded — Modal eval not yet kicked off. This is **the highest-EV un-run experiment in the repo**. P(clears mean val IC > +0.025, 4/5 windows): **45%** — Liu-Tsyvinski's cross-sectional momentum is ~3× equity strength, and the equity null was hypothesized to be cross-sectional-noise-floor-bound. Free data: CryptoCompare. Cost: 0.5 person-days (just run it).
- **Funding-rate cash-and-carry (He-Manela-Ross-von Wachter 2023 SSRN 4301150).** Persistent positive funding (longs pay shorts) on BTC/ETH perps is a *structural* premium, not an arbitraged anomaly — it pays the liquidity provider to the long-leveraged crowd. Recent empirical (ScienceDirect 2025 S2096720925000818) shows the BTC pair on Binance had a *negative* Sharpe Aug 2023–Feb 2024 (the bear unwind paid shorts), so the trade is **regime-conditional**, exactly what `apps/gate` was designed for. Capacity: billions at BTC/ETH. Free data: Binance funding history. Cost: 2-3 person-days (PnL accounting + funding history loader). P(clears net Sharpe > +0.8 across 2021–2026 with at least one 1y subwindow positive): **55%** — this is the single most structurally-persistent edge accessible from free data. Capacity-constrained-edge MEMORY pointer applies directly.
- **Managed futures / TS-momentum (Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012 *JFE* 104:228; Hurst-Ooi-Pedersen 2017 "Century of Evidence" SSRN 2993026).** The strongest *multi-asset* OOS-replicated alpha factor in the literature. **But** the 2011–2020 decade was hostile (the canonical "trend-following winter") and 2022 was a famous comeback; whether 2023–2025 confirms or kills depends on which sub-period you slice. Free data: Stooq daily on continuous futures contracts? **No** — Stooq's futures coverage is shallow and contract-roll convention is hard. This is a "needs a futures data adapter first" arc. Cost: 5-7 person-days (data adapter + walk-forward). P(clears DCA-uncorrelated bar): **35%** — the decay is real but the diversification-with-DCA case is strong.
- **FX carry (Lustig-Roussanov-Verdelhan 2011 *RFS* 24(11):3731).** Post-GFC decayed (Bhattacharya et al. 2022). Skip — `.research-venue-fit.md` already analyzed and recommended against.
- **Commodity term-structure (Erb-Harvey 2006 *FAJ* 62(2):69).** Bhardwaj-Gorton-Rouwenhorst 2015 says the OOS 2005-2014 was consistent with the 1959-2004 in-sample; refined dynamic-curve strategies (ScienceDirect 2023 S0378426623001632) post Sharpe 1.27–1.41 net. Same data adapter problem as managed futures. Cost: 5-7 person-days. P(clears bar): **30%**.
- **Credit (HY-Treasury spread timing).** Free FRED data, weak OOS literature, no clear edge. Skip.
- **Single-stock options skew.** Same paid-data wall the illiquid-VRP arc hit. Skip — unless we accept gauss314 HF + DoltHub coverage limits, in which case `vol_v3` already harvests the strongest piece.
- **Vol-of-vol (VVIX-based).** VVIX is daily and free; the academic OOS evidence is thin (mostly Chicago-school in-sample). Tertiary.

## 3. Structurally-persistent / capacity-constrained edges

These are the closest match to the MEMORY pointer "capacity-constrained / novel-data."

- **Funding-rate carry** — see §2.
- **Congressional trading disclosure (STOCK Act, Ziobrowski 2004 *JFQA* 39:661; Belmont et al. 2025 working paper).** Rank-and-file: null. **Leadership-position members: +40–50pp/year OOS** (Belmont 2025, surfaced in Fortune 2025-12-07 coverage). Free data: quiverquant.com, capitol-trades.com, Senate eFD, House Clerk. Disclosure window: 45-day lag. **Mechanism is regulatory-protected** — committee members see legislative impact information before the public; that information advantage is *legally enforced not to disclose* until after the trade. The trade is "follow the leaders' trades within 45 days of disclosure." Capacity: probably $50–500M (small enough to stay un-arbitraged by funds with reporting requirements). Cost: 5-10 person-days (scraper + signal logic + walk-forward). P(clears non-trivial alpha vs market): **50%** — the most recent academic update is positive. **This is the most novel-to-this-repo edge worth pursuing.**
- **Insider buying / Form 4 (Cohen-Malloy-Pomorski 2012 *JF* 67:1009; arXiv 2602.06198 microcap gradient-boosting 2026).** "Opportunistic" insider purchases predict +3-5% abnormal returns over 6 months; routine purchases are noise. Microcap-specialized recent ML extension (arXiv 2602.06198) confirms persistence. Free data: EDGAR Form 4 XML. **But**: microcap implementation hits the same illiquid-VRP wall — bid-ask + borrow constraints. Restricted to mid/large caps the edge attenuates. Cost: 5-7 person-days. P(clears bar): **30%**.
- **Merger-arbitrage spread (Mitchell-Pulvino 2001 *JF* 56:2135; GMO/Verdad 2024).** Long target, short acquirer if cash+stock; ~6-8% gross annualized historically, ~3-5% net of costs, 89% deal completion rate (2000–2020). 2024 wider spreads due to FTC/Lina-Khan regime. Free data: SEC Form 8-K for deal announcement; daily prices from Stooq. **Operationally complex** — borrow + position sizing per deal; not great for a research repo without a fast scraper. Cost: 10-15 person-days. P(clears DCA-uncorrelated bar): **40%** — durable edge but high build cost.
- **8-K text mining (Feuerriegel et al. 2023 *Decision Support Systems*, ScienceDirect S0167923622001634).** 354k 8-K filings + 10k ad-hoc — 7.81% / 9.34% OOS annualized return from text-analytics-based strategies. Free data: EDGAR. Tinygrad-friendly: TF-IDF + logistic head, no LLM required (the cited result used dictionary-based NLP). Cost: 7-10 person-days. P(clears bar): **25%** — the OOS numbers are noisy and the trade may already be arbitraged by 2025 (the cited evidence runs through ~2019).
- **Spinoff post-event drift (Cusatis-Miles-Woolridge 1993; McConnell-Ovtchinnikov 2004).** Documented +10%/yr 3-year post-spinoff alpha. Capacity-constrained (1-2 spinoffs/month). Free data: 8-K + 10-K. Cost: 5-8 person-days. P(clears bar): **35%** — durable, narrow.
- **Short-interest data.** Requires paid S3 or Markit feed. Skip per constraint.

## 4. Operational / frictional edges

Micro-Sharpe but real-money for the live DCA stack.

- **Tax-loss harvest layer on DCA.** Sell losers > 1-year-old at year-end, buy correlated substitute, harvest the loss vs ordinary income. Documented +0.5%–1.0%/yr at moderate income brackets (Berkin-Ye 2003, Khang-LaBarge-Miller 2019 Vanguard). Cost: 3-5 person-days (Alpaca lot-tracking). P(clears its own bar): **80%** — the math is mechanical; only failure mode is implementation complexity exceeding tolerance.
- **Rebal-day-of-week / month-of-quarter shifting.** Small, well-documented turn-of-the-month effect (Ariel 1987; Lakonishok-Smidt 1988). Decayed but not dead. Cost: 0.5 person-days (sweep). P(clears noise): **20%**.
- **Dividend-reinvestment timing.** Trivially small. Skip.
- **Options-overlay for known events (ex-div/earnings) on DCA basket.** Sell calls on the 13-ETF basket monthly. Documented "BuyWrite premium" (BXM index has Sharpe ≈ 0.4–0.5 vs SPX ≈ 0.5–0.6; comparable risk-adjusted but lower-vol). Operationally heavy. Cost: 10 person-days. P(clears DCA-uncorrelated): **15%** — overlay correlates 0.7+ with DCA underlying.

**Tax-loss harvest is the only one worth implementing.** It's not research, it's engineering.

## 5. Allocator-side research not yet exhausted

The repo just ran 2x meta-allocator falsifications. Going back to the well requires a sharper hypothesis than "Rapach-Strauss-Zhou combination but better."

- **Combination forecasts (Rapach-Strauss-Zhou 2010 *RFS* 23:821).** Already meta-allocator-tested; simple-mean = 1/N which lost to inverse-vol on the 6-arc panel and lost back to 1/N on the 5-arc no-vol_v3 panel. **Verdict: closed.** The 2026-05-24 falsification page makes the call.
- **HRP (López de Prado 2016 *JPM* 42:59).** Hierarchical risk parity; not tested in this repo but pre-supposes a correlation matrix across return streams. With 5 arcs the covariance estimate is unstable (cf. the no-vol_v3 falsification — adding/removing one arc moves the answer). Cost: 1-2 person-days. P(beats inverse-vol-of-arc): **20%** — small-N is the killer.
- **Black-Litterman with macro priors.** Theoretically appealing; in practice requires both a credible prior *and* a credible view-confidence. Welch-Goyal-Zafirov 2024 says we don't have either at <12m horizons. Skip.
- **Michaud resampled efficiency (Michaud 1998).** Bootstrap mean-variance; reduces estimation error but doesn't add information. Same small-N constraint as HRP. Skip.

**Conclusion: allocator-side research is mostly closed for this repo's small-N (5–6 arcs) regime.** Don't open a 7th meta-allocator arc. Build more arcs first.

## 6. Long-horizon predictability for DCA contribution sizing

The DCA charter is fixed-target equal-weight, quarterly rebal. Long-horizon predictability of the equity premium can adjust **savings rate**, not portfolio weights.

- **CAPE / Campbell-Shiller PE.** Strong long-horizon (10y) OOS power (R² ~30%); useless at 1y. Operationally: when CAPE > 30, route incremental savings to bonds; when CAPE < 15, accelerate equity buying. Cost: 0.5 person-days (FRED + threshold logic). P(clears noise): **30%** — but the *operational* benefit (psychological pre-commitment to dollar-cost-averaging *more* in cheap years) outweighs the statistical edge.
- **cay (Lettau-Ludvigson 2001).** Same shape, slightly different signal. P(adds info over CAPE): **15%**.
- **Demographics (Geanakoplos-Magill-Quinzii 2004).** Right-direction, wrong-horizon — operates on 10-30y waves. Too slow.

**Verdict: implement a one-page CAPE-tuned contribution-sizing overlay** as a behavioral pre-commitment device, not as a research arc. Don't measure it as a leaderboard row — it's an operational lever.

## 7. Alternative data accessible for free

- **FRED — beyond the current 6-feature regime stack.** Consumer-sentiment (UMCSENT), jobless-claims surprises (ICSA + Bloomberg consensus diff — but consensus is paid), housing starts (HOUST), ISM (paid for live but FRED has lagged). The macro-regime-diagnostic finding already says 5/6 macro features predict pivot-arc window outcomes; adding more is diminishing returns. Cost: 1 day. P(adds non-trivial info): **20%**.
- **Google Trends.** Recent literature (ScienceDirect 2024 S1057521923000650) repositions Trends as an *uncertainty/attention* measure, not a return predictor. Reversal of earlier optimism. Operationally useful as a regime feature, not as a signal. Cost: 2 days. P(adds info): **20%**.
- **Wikipedia view counts (Moat-Curme-Avakian-Kenett-Stanley-Preis 2013 *Sci. Reports*).** Same family as Google Trends; documented weak OOS predictability of crash probability. Not unique enough. Skip.
- **Congressional trading.** Covered in §3. **This is the single highest-EV alternative-data signal accessible from free sources.**
- **Weather data for ag commodities.** Requires also having ag-futures exposure. Skip (no commodity arc yet).
- **EDGAR-broad text (10-K / 8-K / Form 4 / S-1).** Covered in §3.

## The 3 to run next, in order

### 1. **Crypto factor port — execute the already-scaffolded Modal run.** (0.5 person-days)

The pre-reg, the scaffold, and the smoke-test (`v_IC +0.0504, 4/5 positive`) all exist. The Modal eval costs ~$0.10. **Not running it is the single biggest pending-arc cost on the board.** This is also the cleanest test of "is the equity null cross-sectional-noise-floor-bound or representation-bound?" — a confirmed-OOS here closes a research question that has been open since `apps/factor` was built.

- **Ship if**: mean val IC > +0.025, 4/5 windows positive, DSR t > +1.5 on deployable L/S stream.
- **Kill if**: mean val IC < +0.020 OR < 3/5 windows positive. Pre-reg is locked; do not move the bar.
- **What changes if it ships**: a second confirmed-OOS arc exists beyond DCA + vol_v3, and the next experiment is the **funding-rate cash-and-carry** overlay (candidate #2) on the same crypto universe — they're operationally co-located.

### 2. **Funding-rate cash-and-carry overlay** (2-3 person-days, conditional on #1 shipping OR running in parallel)

The strongest structurally-persistent-edge candidate that matches the MEMORY pointer. The trade is the canonical "small operator harvests the structural premium that capacity-constrained leveraged longs pay." Critically: empirical evidence (ScienceDirect 2025) shows BTC funding turned **negative** in the 2023-2024 bear unwind — confirming this is regime-conditional, not a constant carry. That maps perfectly onto the repo's gate-conditioning machinery; the trade is *predictable-when-not-bear* + *cut-when-funding-flips*.

- **Ship if**: net (after 5bps maker fees + funding accounting) Sharpe > +0.8 across 2021–2026, AND at least one 1y subwindow positive, AND the per-month PnL is < 0.4 correlated with DCA.
- **Kill if**: net Sharpe < +0.4 OR > 0.7 correlation with DCA (then it's just a DCA-on-crypto in disguise).
- **What changes if it ships**: DCA + vol_v3 sleeve + crypto-funding-carry sleeve becomes the canonical live deployment recipe — three uncorrelated streams, two of which are structural premia (equity-risk + funding-paid-by-crowded-long) and one a forecaster (vol_v3).

### 3. **Congressional trading disclosure follower (leadership-only)** (5-10 person-days, novel-data arc)

The single most "novel-to-this-repo" candidate. Belmont et al. 2025's leadership-only result (+40-50pp/year OOS) is the largest published unexploited-on-public-data alpha currently in the literature. The 45-day disclosure lag is exactly the right horizon for our daily-bar infrastructure. The scraper investment also unlocks the EDGAR-text-mining family (#6) and Form 4 (#9) as follow-on arcs.

- **Ship if**: walk-forward 2013-2025 alpha vs SPY > +3% net, top-quartile leadership subset beats rank-and-file subset by > +2% net, AND the trade is implementable with weekly rebal (capacity sanity check via "did the leader's trade exceed our position size").
- **Kill if**: leadership subset does not separate from rank-and-file (Belmont 2025 doesn't replicate) OR top-quartile alpha < +1%. The Belmont result is **one paper, recent, and not yet stress-tested by the broader literature**; verdict logic must be strict.
- **What changes if it ships**: first novel-data arc; opens the EDGAR-broad family as a viable research thread and validates "follow the operator-with-asymmetric-information" as a structural premise the repo can re-use. If it kills, the Belmont 2025 result was likely sample-selection-biased and the repo updates its prior on insider-disclosure alphas downward — which itself is a finding worth recording.

## Bottom line

Three of the candidates (#1 crypto factor port, #2 funding-rate carry, #3 congressional disclosure) are mechanically different: a venue port, a structural carry premium, and a novel-data information edge. Running all three in sequence within ~3 weeks of person-time gives the repo a real chance of having **2–3 uncorrelated production streams alongside DCA** by mid-2026, rather than continuing to grind on cross-sectional equity prediction problems that the leaderboard already says are dead.

The HAR-RV sleeve-sizing (#4) and tax-loss-harvest (#5) candidates are the *engineering* follow-ons — both quick, both ship-likely, both increase live-deployed Sharpe without requiring new research bets. They should be picked up in parallel by anyone not blocked on Modal eval cycles.

Everything else on the table — meta-allocators, allocator refinements, cross-sectional equity factor refinements, vol_v2-style basket searches — is in the diminishing-returns zone for this repo and should be deferred behind these six.
